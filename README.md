# 🧙‍♂️ THE LORD OF THE RINGS: The Quest for Middle-earth

An epic story-driven RPG adventure through J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle-earth! Experience the journey of the Fellowship, battle legendary enemies, and decide the fate of the One Ring!

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

### Opening the Game:

**Method 1: Right-click and Open**
1. Right-click on `lotr-game.html`
2. Select "Open with"
3. Choose your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

**Method 2: Drag and Drop**
1. Open your web browser
2. Drag `lotr-game.html` into the browser window

**Method 3: Browser Open Menu**
1. Open your browser
2. Press Ctrl+O (or Cmd+O on Mac)
3. Find and open `lotr-game.html`

## 🗡️ CONTROLS

**Story Mode:**
- Read the epic narrative
- Make choices that affect your journey
- Click buttons to advance the story

**Combat Mode:**
- **⚔️ Attack** - Strike your enemy
- **🛡️ Defend** - Reduce incoming damage
- **✨ Special** - Use your character's special ability
- **🎒 Use Item** - Consume items from inventory
- **🏃 Flee** - Attempt to escape (50% chance)

## ⚔️ COMBAT SYSTEM

### Enemy Types:

**👹 Orc Scouts** (Easy)
- Health: 50
- Reward: 50 XP
- Drops: Orc Blade, Health Potions

**💀 Nazgûl** (Medium)
- Health: 100
- Special: Fear Aura
- Reward: 200 XP
- Drops: Dark Essence

**🦍 Cave Troll** (Boss)
- Health: 150
- High damage and defense
- Reward: 500 XP
- Drops: Troll Hide, Greater Potions

### Combat Mechanics:

- **Turn-based combat** - You go first!
- **Attack** - Deal damage based on Attack stat
- **Defend** - Reduce next attack by 50%
- **Special Ability** - 2x damage, 3-turn cooldown
- **Items** - Use healing items mid-battle
- **Flee** - 50% escape chance (not from bosses)
