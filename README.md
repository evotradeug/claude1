# 🚀 COSMIC DEFENDER - Epic Space Shooter Game

An action-packed retro space shooter with explosive gameplay, epic boss battles, and tons of power-ups!

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

### Opening the Game:

**Method 1: Right-click and Open**
1. Right-click on `game.html`
2. Select "Open with"
3. Choose your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

**Method 2: Drag and Drop**
1. Open your web browser
2. Drag `game.html` into the browser window

**Method 3: Browser Open Menu**
1. Open your browser
2. Press Ctrl+O (or Cmd+O on Mac)
3. Find and open `game.html`

## 🕹️ CONTROLS

- **⬅️ ➡️ Arrow Keys** - Move your ship left and right
- **⬆️ ⬇️ Arrow Keys** - Move up and down
- **SPACEBAR** - Shoot lasers

That's it! Super simple controls!

## 🎯 GAME FEATURES

### 💥 ENEMIES
- **Red Squares** - Basic enemies (10 points)
- **Purple Triangles** - Fast enemies (20 points)
- **Orange Tanks** - Heavy enemies with more health (50 points)
- **MASSIVE BOSSES** - Appear every 20 kills! (500 points)

### 🎁 POWER-UPS (Collect the colored circles!)
- **🔵 Blue (SHIELD)** - Become invincible for 5 seconds!
- **🔴 Red (RAPID FIRE)** - Shoot super fast for 5 seconds!
- **🟡 Yellow (TRIPLE SHOT)** - Fire 3 bullets at once for 5 seconds!
- **🟢 Green (HEALTH)** - Restore 30 health points!

### 🏆 GAMEPLAY
- **Score points** by destroying enemies
- **Level up** - Each level gets harder and faster
- **Boss battles** every 20 enemies killed
- **High score tracking** - Beat your best score!
- **Health bar** - Don't let it reach zero!

## 💡 TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Keep moving!** - Standing still = getting hit
2. **Collect power-ups** - They make you unstoppable
3. **Save your shield** for boss battles
4. **Learn boss patterns** - They have 3 different attack modes
5. **Dodge enemy bullets** - They shoot back!
6. **Stay near the bottom** - More reaction time to dodge

## 🌟 BOSS BATTLE TIPS

When you see **⚠️ BOSS INCOMING ⚠️**:
- The boss has a health bar above it
- It shoots in 3 different patterns:
  1. **Spray pattern** - Wide spread of bullets
  2. **Aimed shots** - Bullets aimed right at you!
  3. **Circle pattern** - Bullets in all directions
- Defeating a boss gives you 3 power-ups!
- Each boss gets tougher as you level up
